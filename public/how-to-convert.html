How to Convert PDF to DXF Online

PDF2CAD Online helps you convert supported PDF drawings into DXF files that can be opened in compatible CAD software.

Step 1: Open PDF2CAD Online

Visit PDF2CAD Online using your computer browser.

Step 2: Select Your PDF Drawing

Click the PDF upload option and select a PDF drawing from your computer.

Step 3: Start Conversion

Start the conversion process and allow the browser to process your drawing.

Step 4: Download Your DXF File

When conversion is complete, download the generated DXF file to your computer.

Step 5: Open the DXF in CAD Software

Open the downloaded DXF file in AutoCAD or another compatible CAD application. Check dimensions, text, lines and other drawing elements carefully.

Tips for Better Conversion Results

  • Use vector-based PDF drawings whenever possible.
  • Scanned PDFs may not convert into editable vector geometry correctly.
  • Complex drawings may require additional cleanup.
  • Always check the converted drawing before using it in a professional project.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is PDF2CAD Online free?

Yes. The converter is currently free to use.

Can I open DXF files in AutoCAD?

Yes. DXF is a CAD exchange format supported by AutoCAD and many other CAD applications.

Can scanned PDFs be converted?

Results may be limited because scanned PDFs contain images rather than editable vector drawing elements.

Will the converted drawing be exactly the same?

Not always. Results depend on the PDF structure and drawing quality. Always review the output.
