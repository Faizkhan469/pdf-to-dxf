How to Choose the Right Guide

  • If you are new to conversion, start with the PDF to DXF tutorial.
  • If your PDF is a scan, read the scanned drawing guide.
  • If you are unsure about the PDF type, compare vector and scanned PDFs.
  • If your drawing dimensions look wrong, follow the AutoCAD scale tutorial.
  • If conversion results are incomplete, consult the troubleshooting guide.
Important CAD reminder: Converted drawings should always be compared with their original PDF. Verify critical dimensions and inspect geometry before using DXF files in construction, fabrication or professional documentation.
