What Can You Contact Us About?

  • Conversion problems: Report errors when selecting, processing or downloading a PDF drawing.
  • DXF output: Share details about missing lines, text or unexpected conversion results.
  • Website problems: Report pages that do not load correctly or links that are not working.
  • Suggestions: Recommend useful features or improvements for the converter.
  • General questions: Ask questions about using PDF2CAD Online.

Information That Helps Us Understand a Problem

When reporting a technical issue, please include as much relevant information as you can. This can help us understand what happened.

  • A brief description of the problem.
  • The type of PDF drawing you were trying to convert.
  • The browser and operating system you are using.
  • Any error message displayed by the website.
  • The steps you followed before the problem occurred.

You do not need to include every detail. A clear description of the issue is a useful starting point.

Protect Your Privacy

Please do not email confidential architectural drawings, private project documents, passwords or sensitive personal information. Only share information that is necessary to explain your enquiry.

Before using the converter with sensitive documents, review our Privacy Policy and follow your organization's document-handling requirements.
