Privacy Policy

How PDF2CAD Online handles information when you use our website.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PDF2CAD Online respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit our website and use our free PDF-to-DXF conversion tool.

We aim to make the conversion process straightforward and transparent. Please read this policy to understand how the converter works, what technical information may be processed, and how third-party services such as hosting and advertising may be involved.

1. How PDF Files Are Processed

PDF2CAD Online is designed to process selected PDF drawings locally in your web browser. The converter does not intentionally upload your selected PDF files to a separate conversion server.

Your browser and device perform the conversion and generate the downloadable DXF output. The exact behavior may depend on your browser, device, available memory, and network environment.

Important: Although the converter is designed for local browser processing, we cannot guarantee identical behavior in every environment. Avoid using confidential, sensitive, or restricted drawings unless you have independently verified the processing behavior and are comfortable using the service.

2. Information We Collect

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the PDF-to-DXF converter.

However, our website hosting provider may automatically process certain technical information when you visit the website. This may include:

  • IP address and general connection information.
  • Browser type and device information.
  • Pages and resources requested from the website.
  • Access times and basic server or security logs.

Such information may be processed by the hosting provider to deliver website content, maintain availability, detect misuse, and protect the service.

3. Cookies and Advertising

PDF2CAD Online may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, security, or advertising, depending on which services are enabled.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that service and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to display advertisements, measure advertising performance, and provide relevant advertising.

Google's use of advertising cookies may allow Google and its partners to show advertisements based on visits to this website or other websites, subject to the applicable settings and policies.

You can review and manage available Google advertising preferences through Google Ads Settings .

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website or third-party service functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

PDF2CAD Online uses third-party infrastructure to host and deliver the website. These providers may process technical information necessary to operate, maintain, and protect their services.

If third-party advertising, analytics, or other external services are used, those services may handle information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of external websites or services. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services they interact with.

5. Data Security and Limitations

We take reasonable steps to provide a reliable website experience. However, no website, browser, device, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should keep their browsers and devices updated and should avoid using the converter with confidential documents unless they have assessed the risks for their own situation.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF2CAD Online is a general-purpose technical tool and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the converter.

7. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services for additional information. When you follow an external link, you leave PDF2CAD Online and become subject to the privacy practices of that external website.
