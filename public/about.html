About PDF2CAD Online

PDF2CAD Online is a free browser-based tool designed to help architects, CAD draftsmen, engineers and construction professionals convert supported PDF drawings into DXF files.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make PDF-to-DXF conversion simple and accessible. Select a PDF drawing, process it in your browser and download the resulting DXF file for compatible CAD software.

Who Can Use PDF2CAD Online?

  • Architects and architectural draftsmen
  • Civil and CAD engineers
  • Construction professionals
  • Students learning CAD

How It Works

  1. Select a PDF drawing from your device.
  2. Start the conversion.
  3. Download the generated DXF file.
  4. Review the drawing in your CAD software.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide a simple conversion experience. Results may vary depending on PDF structure and drawing quality. Always review converted files before using them in professional projects.
